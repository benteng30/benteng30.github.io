Case study · Beeken Biomedical · Fall 2026

Finding the bottleneck in a medical-device supply chain

  1. Map all eight stages of the chain
  2. Put every stage in the same unit
  3. Find the one step that sets the pace
  4. Model three demand scenarios and build a 90-day plan

Beeken Biomedical, the company behind NuStat hemostatic gauze, needed to know if its supply chain could keep up as demand grew.

What I did: As the team's data analyst, I analyzed demand, sales, inventory, and production data to pinpoint bottlenecks, and modeled 2x–3x demand-growth scenarios behind the team's recommendations.

Result: We showed that one production step, not demand, set the limit, and gave Beeken's leadership a 90-day plan with an owner and date for each action.
